Third-Party Risk Assessment Case Study

This project is a portfolio case study designed to mirror the work of a junior third-party risk or GRC analyst.

Assessment workflow

  • Define vendor profile and service criticality.
  • Review evidence such as SOC 2 report, policies, access control documentation, and incident response procedures.
  • Identify gaps and assign likelihood, impact, and risk rating.
  • Create remediation recommendations and track owner/status.
