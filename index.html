About

Cybersecurity with a risk-first mindset.

My interest in cybersecurity started long before I knew what cybersecurity actually was. Growing up, I watched way too many crime shows and was always curious about how investigators figured things out from digital evidence. That curiosity eventually led me to cybersecurity.

During college, I realized the field was much bigger than I originally thought. While I enjoy the technical side of security, I became especially interested in risk, compliance, governance, and understanding how organizations make security decisions. Through this website, I share projects, articles, lessons learned, and whatever else I'm currently exploring in cybersecurity.
