Risk Classification

Vendor tiering model

Vendor risk tiers determine assessment depth, review frequency, and monitoring requirements.

Tier 1

Critical

Risk Score: 15–25
  • Full SIG questionnaire
  • SOC 2 Type II review
  • Annual reassessment
  • Real-time monitoring

Tier 2

High

Risk Score: 8–14
  • SIG Lite questionnaire
  • SOC 2 Type I or II accepted
  • Biennial assessment
  • Quarterly monitoring

Tier 3

Moderate / Low

Risk Score: 1–7
  • Security questionnaire
  • DPA if PII involved
  • Annual security rating review
  • Lower due diligence scope
