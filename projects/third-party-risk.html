Assessment context

This fictional vendor profile is used to demonstrate a structured third-party risk review process.

Data Access

Sensitive Business Data

Criticality

Moderate to High

Findings Matrix

Control gaps identified

High

MFA Not Enforced for Privileged Accounts

Privileged users could access sensitive systems without enforced multi-factor authentication.

Require MFA implementation and completion evidence.
Medium

BCP / DR Test Evidence Not Provided

Vendor did not provide recent evidence showing business continuity or disaster recovery testing.

Request updated BCP/DR testing documentation.
