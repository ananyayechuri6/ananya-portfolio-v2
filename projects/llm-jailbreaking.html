Threat Landscape

Where AI security risks can appear

Large Language Models introduce new risk areas because their outputs are shaped by user inputs, context, system instructions, and safety controls.

AI Security Risks

Hallucinations

Confident but inaccurate responses.

Model Manipulation

Shaping outputs through crafted inputs.

Social Engineering

Using AI to assist deceptive tactics.
